# Physical-ledger audit — synthetic simulation experiment

## Scope and design

A software correctness campaign, **not** an empirical economic experiment. Four scenario modes—legacy baseline, historical-cost capital economy, savings-backed credit/capital economy, and a seeded Hybrid World Genesis economy—were simulated independently at 120 agents. Five different random seeds (1,2,3,5,8), each with 12 abstract periods, gave 20 runs and 240 audited periods. Differences across modes are not causal comparisons because their initial endowments and parameters are different.

The engine performed cash and physical audit after every traced period, and the research runner repeated both auditors explicitly from captured opening snapshots. It recorded transaction counts and distinct SHA-256 fingerprints for every period's three event streams, then computed a deterministic experiment manifest. Independent verification fully **reran all 20 simulations** and compared the manifest byte-for-byte after canonical encoding.

## Completed result

- **20** synthetic economy runs; **240** periods.
- **14,116** individually replayed physical events.
- **24,814** individually replayed cash transfers.
- **0** identity failures in the checked money, credit, goods, resources, firm cash-flow, household cash-flow and capital metrics.
- **228** automated tests passed, including existing historical regression checks and 22 new physical/research tests. Exact test count is the local execution at this checkpoint, not a CI claim.
- The independent second execution produced the identical manifest digest: `e5edd2a6941be0b46976f116b29e7f160e980a13abd7d20569eda3a69e25b6c0`.

Reproduction (Python 3.11+, NumPy, pytest):

```bash
PYTHONPATH=src:. PYTEST_DISABLE_PLUGIN_AUTOLOAD=1 pytest -q
PYTHONPATH=src:. python research/run_physical_closure.py --output research/v02_physical_ledger_20
PYTHONPATH=src:. python research/run_physical_closure.py --output research/v02_physical_ledger_20 --verify-only
PYTHONPATH=src:. python research/run_physical_closure.py --output research/v02_physical_ledger_20 --verify-replay
```

The exact per-period evidence hashes and aggregate counts are in [`research/v02_physical_ledger_20/summary.json`](v02_physical_ledger_20/summary.json). Event streams are deterministically regenerated by the runner; `physical_transactions.jsonl` is also emitted by the normal CLI when trace mode is enabled.

## Interpretation and limitations

These results establish consistency of the recorded physical transactions with balances and a subset of economic accounting metrics for the sampled configurations and horizons. They **do not** establish full economic realism, full coverage of int64 arithmetic extremes, flawless application logic, empirical calibration, dynamic firm failure, or an unqualified guarantee for untested parameter ranges. More complex input-output flows and balance sheets remain open development requirements.
